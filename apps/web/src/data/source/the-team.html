About The Architect

Greetings! I’m the Architect, the visionary behind The Last Block Benders and Block3AI.com. I like to think of myself as a humble creator with a mission to merge technology, education, and philanthropy into a single, meaningful movement.

Who am I?

After many years of seeking certain truths in life, committing hours to meditation, studying and practicing ancient esoteric practices, I stumbled upon the wild world of decentralization, self-custody, cryptocurrency, blockchain, and Web3 technology... and my life was never the same. Soon to follow was the widespread emergence of AI. What started as a fascination with new technology quickly evolved into a deep passion for making it accessible and helping others along the way. I saw an opportunity to go beyond the noise of speculation and create something real—something that would not only educate but also give back to the world.

Enter The Last Block Benders (TLBB): a Block3AI.com project where creativity, community, and purpose come together to bend blocks and shape the future for good.
